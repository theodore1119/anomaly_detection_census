[census_data](census_data.csv): This Excel spreadsheet extracts the 2024 American Community Survey 5-year estimates of all census tracts across the U.S. It includes the following variables: median household income, per capita income, median home value, and percentage of residents aged 25 and over with Bachelor's degree or above.

[anomaly_detection](anomaly_detection.ipynb): This notebook illustrates how I use the Isolation Forest algorithm to detect anomalies in census data. It explores the 2024 American Community Survey (ACS) 5-year data to identify the census tracts that have exceptionally high or low economic well-being--- which I consider as anomalies.
